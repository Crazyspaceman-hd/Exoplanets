# Exoplanets

This repo is old and that's the point, it represents pre-AI work.

It's not glamourous and I would do so much of it differently now, 
(the data never updating bothers me to no end) but it answers questions I had about a field I was interested in.

Are we still discovering planets?
Yes all the time, the universe appears to be just lousy with planets

How do we discover planets?
Transit mostly! Literally measuring the planet move across the disc of the star, a close runner up is radial wobble and very interesting technique where we measure the gravity of the planet pulling on the host star making it appear to 'wobble'

Which telescopes do the most planet finding?
The Kepler Space telescope by a huge margin! Mostly likely due to the fact it was purpose built for the task, but at the time ground telescopes were still ahead of total discoveries.
